About

The Vantage was created to examine complex subjects from an unfazed, unvarnished perspective, which is where the name comes from. In an environment where public discourse is often shaped by partisan loyalties, commercial pressures, or surface-level commentary, this platform exists to look directly at the structural realities affecting Nepal and the wider region without flinching or softening the argument.

Publishing through established media outlets often means trading away depth, nuance, and intellectual independence to fit institutional boundaries or advertiser sensitivities. Building an independent hub was a deliberate decision to bypass those constraints entirely, ensuring that long-form essays on national infrastructure, energy sovereignty, buried history, and sports governance remain accountable only to the truth and to the Nepali readers they are written for.

Rather than restricting itself to a single beat, The Vantage follows ideas wherever the evidence leads. Whether breaking down the economics of Nepali hydropower, challenging sanitized historical narratives, or analyzing the administrative failures holding back domestic sports, every piece is written with the conviction that Nepali readers deserve serious, uncompromising analysis.

The platform is written and maintained by Yogesh Pokharel. Originally from Birtamode, Jhapa, Nepal, he is currently based in Kathmandu, where he works as an IELTS instructor and pursues his interest in songwriting alongside writing on diverse subjects.
