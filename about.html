ABOUT

An Unfazed Perspective: Why The Vantage Exists

The Vantage is an independent Nepali publication dedicated to exploring policy, economics, history, and sports with depth, clarity, and an unvarnished perspective, cutting through partisan noise and surface-level commentary.

Why an Independent Hub?

Free from institutional pressure and editorial gatekeeping, the platform offers long-form essays and rigorous inquiry tailored for a curious audience in Nepal.

What The Vantage Covers

  • Policy & Economics: Structural analysis on infrastructure, energy, and youth migration.
  • History: Essays challenging conventional South Asian historical narratives.
  • Sports: Critical reporting on Nepali sports administration and athlete realities.

About the Founder

Maintained by Yogesh Pokharel, a Kathmandu-based writer and instructor originally from Birtamode, Jhapa.
